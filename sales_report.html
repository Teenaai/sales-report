The discount problem

Profit by discount band

Bars above the line are profitable, below the line are losses. Every band above 20% is negative.

Every band above 20% loses money. Those bands carry only 15.8% of sales ($362,770.15) yet destroy $135,376.06 — nearly half of the $286,397.02 total profit. Across the whole file, 1,871 loss-making line items (18.7%) average a 48.1% discount and turn $468,707.15 of sales into a $156,131.29 loss.

Recommendations

1
Cap discretionary discounts at 20%
Every band above 20% is loss-making; those bands destroy $135,376.06 while carrying only 15.8% of sales.
2
Reprice or delist Tables, Bookcases and Supplies
The three loss-making sub-categories give up $22,387.14 of profit on $368,519.07 of sales.
3
Put Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois on discount review
Together they lose $70,868.59; Texas alone loses $25,729.36 on $170,188.05 of sales.
4
Grow Copiers, Paper and Accessories
Copiers earn $55,617.82 at a 37.2% margin from only 68 orders — the highest-yield line in the dataset.
5
Protect Q4 margin, not just Q4 volume
Q4 2017 hit a record $280,054.07 at 9.8% margin versus 16.2% the year before.
